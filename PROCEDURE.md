# Tagging Procedure

## What should be tagged

Any phrase that identifies an identifiable person should be tagged, including:
* Proper names (`When \zwd Jesus|Q302\zwd* arrived`)
* Specific non-name nouns and noun phrases (`search carefully for \zwd the Child|Q302\zwd*`)

Include any words that are part of the identification of the person, such as:
* `\zwd Simon called Peter|Q33923\zwd*`
* `spoken of by \zwd the prophet Daniel|Q171724\zwd*`
* 'the' (`but \zwd the Son of Man|Q302\zwd* has no place to lay His head`)

Do **not** tag:
* Pronouns
* Titles, when the title is used to refer to a position rather than a specific person (`What do you think about the Christ?`, `Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not?`)
* Unidentifiable people (`...invited a little child to stand among them.`, `...sent out two disciples`)

Do **not** include additional words that describe the person that are part of a separate phrase, such as:
* `This is \zwd Jesus|Q302\zwd*, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.`
* ` \zwd Judas|Q81018\zwd*, one of the Twelve`

Angels may be tagged if identifiable.

## Complicated cases

Tags may need to be nested, such as:
* When a person is identified by their relationship to someone else (`\zwd\zwd Uriah|Q71267\zwd*’s wife|Q272277\zwd*`, `\zwd James son of \zwd Zebedee|Q169363\zwd*|Q43399\zwd*`)

Sometimes the same phrase may refer to multiple identifiable people, such as:
* `“We can,” \zwd the brothers|Q43999,Q44015\zwd* answered.`

If the exact identity of a person is unclear or they lack a Wikidata item, use a blank tag (`Now the \zwd the king of Egypt|\zwd* did not march out`).
