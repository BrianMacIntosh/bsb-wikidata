# Tagging Procedure

## What should be tagged

Any phrase that identifies an identifiable person should be tagged, including:
* Proper names (`When \zwd Jesus|id="Q302"\zwd* arrived`)
* Specific non-name nouns and noun phrases (`search carefully for \zwd the Child|id="Q302"\zwd*`)

Include any words that are part of the identification of the person, such as:
* `\zwd Simon called Peter|id="Q33923"\zwd*`
* `spoken of by \zwd the prophet Daniel|id="Q171724"\zwd*`
* 'the' (`but \zwd the Son of Man|id="Q302"\zwd* has no place to lay His head`)

Do **not** tag:
* Pronouns
* Titles, when the title is used to refer to a position rather than a specific person (`What do you think about the Christ?`, `Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not?`)
* Unidentifiable people (`...invited a little child to stand among them.`, `...sent out two disciples`)

Do **not** include additional words that describe the person that are part of a separate phrase, such as:
* `This is \zwd Jesus|id="Q302"\zwd*, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.`
* ` \zwd Judas|id="Q81018"\zwd*, one of the Twelve`

Angels may be tagged if identifiable.

## Complicated cases

Tags may need to be nested, such as:
* When a person is identified by their relationship to someone else (`\zwd\zwd Uriah|id="Q71267"\zwd*’s wife|id="Q272277"\zwd*`, `\zwd James son of \zwd Zebedee|id="Q169363"\zwd*|id="Q43399"\zwd*`)

Sometimes the same phrase may refer to multiple identifiable people, such as:
* `“We can,” \zwd the brothers|id="Q43999,Q44015"\zwd* answered.`

If the exact identity of a person is unclear or they lack a Wikidata item, use a blank tag (`Now the \zwd the king of Egypt|id=""\zwd* did not march out`).
