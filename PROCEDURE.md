# Tagging Procedure

## What should be tagged

Any phrase that identifies an identifiable person should be tagged, including:
* Proper names (`When \x-wd Jesus|id="Q302"\x-wd* arrived`)
* Specific non-name nouns and noun phrases (`search carefully for \x-wd the Child|id="Q302"\x-wd*`)

Include any words that are part of the identification of the person, such as:
* `\x-wd Simon called Peter|id="Q33923"\x-wd*`
* `spoken of by \x-wd the prophet Daniel|id="Q171724"\x-wd*`
* 'the' (`but \x-wd the Son of Man|id="Q302"\x-wd* has no place to lay His head`)

Do **not** tag:
* Pronouns
* Titles, when the title is used to refer to a position rather than a specific person (`What do you think about the Christ?`, `Is it lawful to pay taxes to Caesar or not?`)
* Unidentifiable people (`\x-wd Jesus|id="Q302"\x-wd* invited a little child to stand among them.`, `\x-wd Jesus|id="Q302"\x-wd* sent out two disciples`)

Do **not** include additional words that describe the person that are part of a separate phrase, such as:
* `This is \x-wd Jesus|id="Q302"\x-wd*, the prophet from Nazareth in Galilee.`
* ` \x-wd Judas|id="Q81018"\x-wd*, one of the Twelve`

Angels may be tagged if identifiable.

## Complicated cases

Sometimes tags may need to be nested, such as:
* When a person is identified by their relationship to someone else (`\x-wd\x-wd Uriah|id="Q71267"\x-wd*’s wife|id="Q272277"\x-wd*`, `\x-wd James son of \x-wd Zebedee|id="Q169363"\x-wd*|id="Q43399"\x-wd*`)

Sometimes the same phrase may refer to multiple identifiable people, such as:
* `“We can,” \x-wd the brothers|id="Q43999,Q44015"\x-wd* answered.`
